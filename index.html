The game

The trainer's life, every week of the year

Train

Plan each horse's week, build fitness without boiling them over, and watch for sore shins and coughs. Your assistant trainer helps as much or as little as you like.

Ride

Choose a running style, break well from the stalls and pick the moment to kick for home. Over jumps, time each leap yourself.

Race

Entries, declarations, the draw and the going. Official ratings and handicaps that work like the real thing, from Class 6 to Group 1.

Breed

Real coat colour genetics, a speed gene that sets each horse's trip, and a stallion roster whose fees rise and fall on their foals' results.

Buy and sell

Bid in guineas at the yearling, horses-in-training and foal sales against rival buyers with deep pockets.

Build an empire

Staff, owners, facilities and the bank. Grow from a handful of boxes to a yard the whole sport talks about.
